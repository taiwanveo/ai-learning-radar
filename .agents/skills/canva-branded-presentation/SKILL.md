# Canva Branded Presentation & Pitch Deck Skill

Use this skill when preparing structured pitch decks, branded slides, or Canva presentation layouts.

## Standard 10-Slide Investor Pitch Deck Structure

1. **Title Slide**: Product Name, Tagline, Presenter Name/Title, Logo.
2. **Problem**: Clear description of the customer pain point, current inefficiencies, market friction.
3. **Solution / Product**: Value proposition, core product capabilities, screenshot/demo visuals.
4. **Market Opportunity (TAM/SAM/SOM)**: Market size ($), growth rate (CAGR), target customer profile.
5. **Business Model**: How the company makes money (SaaS pricing, transaction fee, enterprise tier, unit economics).
6. **Traction & Milestones**: Key metrics (ARR, MoM Growth, Active Users, Key Partnerships, Customers).
7. **Competitive Landscape & Moat**: Differentiation matrix, unfair advantage, IP, network effects.
8. **Go-to-Market Strategy (GTM)**: Acquisition channels, sales motion, expansion strategy.
9. **Financial Projections & Key Metrics**: 3-year revenue forecast, gross margin %, key assumptions.
10. **Team & The Ask**: Founders/Leadership backgrounds, funding amount requested, use of funds breakdown.

## Brand Kit Rules & Visual Guidelines

- **Typography Rule**: Pair 1 Header Font (Bold, High Personality like Montserrat/Outfit/Poppins) with 1 Body Font (Clean, Readable like Inter/Roboto/Open Sans).
- **Color Rule (60-30-10 Rule)**:
  - 60% Dominant Background Color (Light or Dark).
  - 30% Secondary Structure Color (Cards, Containers, Text).
  - 10% High-Impact Accent Color (CTAs, Key Metrics, Highlights).
- **Density Limit**: Maximum 30 words per slide. Use visual cards, icons, and big stat numbers instead of paragraphs.
- **Aspect Ratio**: Standard 16:9 Widescreen.

## Export & Delivery Options

1. **HTML Interactive Slides**: Self-contained HTML presentation with slide navigation (Arrow keys, progress bar, responsive layout).
2. **Canva Specification Sheet**: Markdown structure with explicit design instructions per slide (Colors, fonts, graphic elements, layout grid) ready to build or import into Canva.
3. **PowerPoint (.pptx)**: Structured presentation format editable in Microsoft PowerPoint or Google Slides.
