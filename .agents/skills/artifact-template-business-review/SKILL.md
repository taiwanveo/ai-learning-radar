# Business Review (QBR / ABR) Artifact Template

Use this skill whenever creating a Quarterly Business Review (QBR), Annual Business Review (ABR), or executive performance update artifact.

## Core Sections of a Business Review

1. **Executive Overview & Highlights**
   - Top 3 accomplishments of the quarter / year.
   - Summary score against primary OKRs/KPIs (On Track / At Risk / Behind).

2. **Financial & Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
   - Revenue, ARR/MRR, Gross Margin, CAC, LTV, Net Retention Rate (NRR), Churn Rate.
   - Target vs. Actual performance comparison table with variance % and status indicators.

3. **Product & Operational Milestones**
   - Delivered features vs. planned roadmap.
   - Operational efficiency improvements, customer satisfaction (NPS/CSAT) metrics.

4. **Strategic Initiatives Progress**
   - Key projects update: Timeline, owner, budget status, key deliverables.

5. **Risks, Blockers & Mitigation Plan**
   - High-impact risks identified, probability/impact rating, owner, and action steps.

6. **Next Period Goals & Action Plan**
   - Top 3-5 priorities for upcoming quarter, resource requirements, key dependencies.

## Standard Business Review Table Schema

| Metric / Initiative | Q1 Target | Q1 Actual | Variance | Status | Owner |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| ARR ($M) | $5.0M | $5.3M | +6.0% | 🟢 On Track | Finance |
| Net Revenue Retention | 115% | 118% | +3.0% | 🟢 On Track | Sales |
| Customer Acquisition Cost | $450 | $520 | -15.5% | 🟡 Warning | Marketing |
| Feature Release v2.4 | Q1 W10 | Q1 W12 | +2 Wks | 🔴 Delayed | Product |

## Design Rules for Executive Reviews

- Use **Traffic Light Colors** consistently:
  - 🟢 Green (`#22c55e` / `#16a34a`): On Track / Exceeded
  - 🟡 Yellow (`#eab308` / `#ca8a04`): Minor Delay / At Risk
  - 🔴 Red (`#ef4444` / `#dc2626`): Off Track / Requires Escalation
- Keep executive summary punchy: maximum 1 slide or 1 section per key domain.
- All numbers must include context (Period-over-Period, Year-over-Year, or Target vs Actual).
